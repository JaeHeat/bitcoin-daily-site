Bitcoin Price on Thanksgiving, Every Year Since 2010

By Josh Molnar · November 2026 · 5 min read

Somebody at the table is going to ask about Bitcoin on Thursday. They ask every year. Some years it is “should I buy some?” This year it will sound more like “so, is that thing dead yet?” Before you answer, look at the Bitcoin price on Thanksgiving for every year since 2010. I pulled all sixteen. The list is more honest than any opinion at the table, and there is a pattern in the bad years that most people miss.

Bitcoin price on Thanksgiving, year by year

These are end-of-day prices, rounded. Exchanges printed slightly different numbers in the early years, so treat the old ones as close, not exact.

  • 2010. About 28 cents.
  • 2011. About $2.44.
  • 2012. About $12.
  • 2013. About $1,000.
  • 2014. About $370. Down 63% from the year before.
  • 2015. About $356. Down again, by about 4%.
  • 2016. About $737.
  • 2017. About $8,070.
  • 2018. About $4,300. Down 47%.
  • 2019. About $7,440.
  • 2020. About $17,100.
  • 2021. About $59,000.
  • 2022. About $16,600. Down 72%.
  • 2023. About $37,300.
  • 2024. About $95,700. The highest Thanksgiving on record.
  • 2025. About $91,300. Down about 5%.

Fifteen times, Bitcoin showed up to Thanksgiving with a year of history behind it. Ten times it was higher than the year before. Five times it was lower.

Where the Bitcoin price sits this Thanksgiving

As I write this, a few days before the holiday, Bitcoin is trading near $60,000. That is about 34% below last Thanksgiving and less than half of the record high it set in October 2025.

Price would have to jump more than 50% by Thursday to beat last year. So 2026 is almost certainly going on the list as a down year. That makes two lower Thanksgivings in a row, which has happened only once before, in 2014 and 2015.

Here is the part that stings. Thanksgiving 2021 was about $59,000. Five years later, the price at the table is almost exactly the same. Anyone who bought that week and held has gone nowhere.

I am not going to tell you it looks fine. The trend is down, and it has been for more than a year.

The pattern hiding in the bad Thanksgivings

Read the three ugliest years on the list again. 2014. 2018. 2022. Each one is four years after the last. Count four more and you land on 2026.

The brutal Thanksgivings are not random. They show up on a schedule, and this year was on it. I walked through why in the Bitcoin four year cycle.

Now look at what came next each time.

  • After the lows of 2014 and 2015, Thanksgiving 2016 was about double.
  • After 2018, Thanksgiving 2019 was up 73%.
  • After 2022, Thanksgiving 2023 was up 125%.

Be careful with that. Three examples is a tiny pile of evidence. It is history, not a promise. And notice 2015 on that list. The first bad Thanksgiving was not the bottom. The next one was lower still. A bad year at the table does not tell you the falling is over. If you want the timeline, I laid it out in how long Bitcoin bear markets last.

What $100 of Bitcoin every Thanksgiving turned into

Let’s pick a terrible place to start. Thanksgiving 2017 was about three weeks before that cycle’s top. Say you put in $100 that day, then $100 every Thanksgiving through 2025. Nine buys. $900 total.

At today’s price near $60,000, that stack is worth about $4,000.

Here is the honest half. At the high in October 2025, the same stack was worth about $8,400. You would have watched more than $4,000 disappear in thirteen months and done nothing about it. That is what holding actually feels like. The math on buying a little on a schedule is in Bitcoin dollar cost averaging.

What to say when Bitcoin comes up at Thanksgiving

You do not need to win the argument. You need three true sentences.

  1. It is down, and you can say so. About a third lower than last Thanksgiving, and the same price as five years ago.
  2. It has been here before. Bitcoin has shown up to this dinner down 47%, down 63% and down 72%. It was higher the following year or the year after, every time so far.
  3. Nobody knows next year’s number. Not me, not your uncle, not the guy on TV. History leans one way. It does not sign a contract.

And if someone at the table wants to buy, the only rule worth passing along is the boring one. Never put in money you will need back soon.

The price at the table changes every year. The question never does.

Common questions

What was the Bitcoin price on Thanksgiving 2025?

About $91,300. That was roughly 5% lower than Thanksgiving 2024.

What was the highest Bitcoin price on Thanksgiving?

About $95,700 on Thanksgiving 2024. It is the highest Thanksgiving price on record so far.

What was the Bitcoin price on Thanksgiving 2010?

About 28 cents. That was the first Thanksgiving Bitcoin had a market price.

Is Bitcoin usually higher or lower than the previous Thanksgiving?

Higher. From 2011 through 2025, Bitcoin was above the prior Thanksgiving ten times and below it five times.

How much has Bitcoin dropped since last Thanksgiving?

As of November 21, 2026, Bitcoin is near $60,000. That is about 34% below the roughly $91,300 it traded at on Thanksgiving 2025.
