Bitcoin in December: Is the Santa Rally Real?

By Josh Molnar · November 2026 · 5 min read

Every year around this time, the same promise shows up. The Santa rally is coming. Just hold on until Christmas and Bitcoin will hand you a present. So I checked every year on record for Bitcoin in December. That is sixteen Decembers, from 2010 through 2025. Bitcoin finished the month higher in eight of them and lower in the other eight. The most famous holiday rally in crypto is a coin flip.

Bitcoin in December, all sixteen years

Here is the whole record, with nothing left out. Each number is how much Bitcoin gained or lost from the first day of December to the last.

  • 2010 was up 44%
  • 2011 was up 59%
  • 2012 was up 8%
  • 2013 was down 35%
  • 2014 was down 15%
  • 2015 was up 14%
  • 2016 was up 31%
  • 2017 was up 39%
  • 2018 was down 7%
  • 2019 was down 5%
  • 2020 was up 48%
  • 2021 was down 19%
  • 2022 was down 4%
  • 2023 was up 12%
  • 2024 was down 3%
  • 2025 was down 3%

Add them all up and the average December is a gain of about 10%. That sounds great until you see where it comes from. A handful of huge years do all the lifting. Line up all sixteen from worst to best and the one in the middle is a gain of roughly 2%. That is the honest picture of a normal December.

Look at the recent ones too. Four of the last five Decembers finished red. The only green one was 2023. You can see every month side by side on the live monthly returns chart.

Is the Bitcoin Santa rally real?

When most people say Santa rally, they mean the last stretch of the year. So I measured exactly that. I took the price on Christmas Eve and compared it to the price on New Year’s Eve, for all sixteen years.

Bitcoin was higher by New Year’s Eve in only six of those sixteen years. It was lower in ten.

It gets worse the closer you get to today. In the nine years from 2017 through 2025, Christmas week finished red eight times. The single exception was 2020, when Bitcoin jumped 22% in seven days.

So where did the legend come from? The early years. Christmas week gained 21% in 2010, 20% in 2011 and 12% in 2013. Those were real. They were also a long time ago, when Bitcoin was tiny and moved like it. People are still waiting on a gift that mostly stopped arriving nine years ago.

What decides whether Bitcoin goes up in December

The month on the calendar does not move the price. Where Bitcoin already sits in its bigger cycle does.

The great Decembers all came in the middle of a strong run. The years 2016, 2017 and 2020 were already climbing hard before December started. The month just kept the party going.

December is also where big moves have ended. Bitcoin peaked above $1,100 in the first week of December 2013, then lost 35% by the end of that same month. It peaked again just under $20,000 in the middle of December 2017. One year later, in the middle of December 2018, the long crash finally found its low near $3,200. Even that December still closed down 7%.

Two patterns stand out when I sort the years.

  • The year after a major top. Those were 2014, 2018 and 2022. December was down 15%, down 7% and down 4%. All three were red.
  • After a red November. November has closed lower six times. December followed it lower in five of them. The one exception was 2011.

Three years and six years are small numbers. I would never call either one a law. But they point the same direction, and it is not the direction the Santa rally crowd is hoping for. I found the same thing when I looked at Bitcoin in November. The calendar gets swallowed by the bigger trend every time.

What the data says about December 2026

Bitcoin is trading near $60,760 as I write this. November is down about 22% with four days left in the month. The daily trend is pointing down. The fear and greed index reads 11, which is extreme fear.

Bitcoin’s last major top came in October 2025. That makes 2026 the year after a top, the same seat that 2014, 2018 and 2022 sat in. And unless the next four days erase a 22% drop, this December follows a red November.

Both patterns put this December in the weaker group. That is not a promise it closes red. History gets a vote, not the final say. But anyone telling you a Santa rally is due is quoting a legend, not the record.

There is one more honest note. A red December and an important low are not opposites. In 2018 the month closed lower and still held the bottom of the entire crash. I am not saying that is happening now. I am saying one month’s color tells you very little about what comes after it.

Common questions

Is December a good month for Bitcoin?

It is a coin flip. Bitcoin finished December higher in 8 of 16 years from 2010 through 2025 and lower in the other 8, and four of the last five Decembers closed red.

Does Bitcoin have a Santa rally?

Not reliably. From Christmas Eve to New Year's Eve, Bitcoin rose in only 6 of 16 years, and that week finished red in 8 of the 9 years from 2017 through 2025.

What is Bitcoin's average return in December?

The average December is a gain of about 10%, but a few huge years do the lifting. The December in the middle of the pack gained roughly 2%.

What was Bitcoin's worst December?

December 2013, when Bitcoin fell about 35% after peaking above $1,100 in the first week of the month. The best was December 2011, up 59%.

Does Bitcoin go up in December after a red November?

Usually not. November has closed lower six times, and December followed it lower in five of them. The one exception was 2011.
