Best Day of the Week to Buy Bitcoin? I Tested All 7

By Josh Molnar · November 2026 · 5 min read

Search for the best day of the week to buy Bitcoin and you walk into a fight. One study crowns Monday. Another says Tuesday. A third swears by Sunday night. They cannot all be right. So I stopped reading and ran the test myself. Seven buyers, seven days, five years of real prices. The winner beat the loser by $123.

The best day of the week to buy Bitcoin, tested on all seven

Here is the setup. Seven people each put $100 into Bitcoin once a week, from November 22, 2021 to November 22, 2026. That is 261 buys and $26,100 each. The only thing that changes is the day. One person always buys on Monday, one always on Tuesday, and so on.

Every buy uses the price at the end of the day. A day here ends at midnight on the world clock, which is early evening in New York. Then I valued every stack at today’s price, about $60,700.

  • Saturday. $36,951
  • Friday. $36,941
  • Sunday. $36,916
  • Thursday. $36,905
  • Monday. $36,875
  • Tuesday. $36,856
  • Wednesday. $36,828

First place to last place is $123. On $26,100. Over five years. That works out to about 47 cents a week. You lose more than that in the couch.

Why every study names a different day

Because the winner keeps changing. I checked which day rose the most, on average, in each year since 2015.

  • 2015 went to Thursday.
  • 2016 and 2018 went to Saturday.
  • 2019 went to Friday.
  • 2021 and 2025 went to Wednesday.
  • 2023 went to Tuesday.
  • 2017, 2020, 2022, 2024 and 2026 so far went to Monday.

Six different days have worn the crown in twelve years. Whoever writes the study just picks up whichever day happened to be winning when they looked.

Monday has the most wins, which is why it gets the headlines. Since 2015, Monday has gained about 0.45% on average, more than any other day. But Bitcoin finished higher on only 53% of those Mondays. That is a coin flip, with a handful of giant days dragging the average up.

Thursday tells the same story backwards. From 2015 through 2020, Bitcoin closed higher on 53% of Thursdays. Since 2021 it is 45%. Same day. Opposite result.

Then there is Saturday. Saturday won my five-year test. So I stretched the same test back to the start of 2017, and Saturday came in dead last. Even there, the best day and the worst day ended up about 1.5% apart after almost ten years of buying.

When the champion of one test is the loser of the next, you are not looking at a pattern. You are looking at luck.

What if you picked the perfect day every week?

Let’s cheat. Give an eighth buyer a time machine. Every single week, for all 261 weeks, they buy on whichever day turned out to be the cheapest.

That stack is worth $38,179. It beats Saturday by about $1,230, or roughly 3%. That is the most a perfect crystal ball could have added in five years.

Now flip it. The unluckiest buyer possible, who somehow picks the most expensive day of every week, ends up with $35,684.

So the whole distance from perfect to cursed is about $2,500 on $26,100. And nobody gets either one. In real life you land in the middle, a few dollars from everybody else.

Compare that with what Bitcoin does on its own. On a normal day it moves about 2%. Inside a typical week, the highest and lowest daily prices sit more than 5% apart. The day you pick is noise next to the week you skip.

What matters more than the day you buy Bitcoin

If the day is worth 47 cents a week, here is where the real money is.

  1. Showing up. All seven buyers turned $26,100 into about $36,900. They did it by buying 261 times in a row, including the weeks when it felt terrible. The math is in Bitcoin dollar cost averaging.
  2. Fees. A 1% fee on every buy costs $261 over those five years. That is more than double the gap between the best day and the worst day. Check what your app charges before you check the calendar.
  3. The bigger calendar. Months have been lumpier than days. I ran that test in the best month to buy Bitcoin, and you can see every month on the Bitcoin monthly returns chart.
  4. A day you will actually keep. The day after payday beats any day a study picked, because the money is there and the habit is easy.

One honest note, because I will not sell you comfort. That $36,900 stack was worth about $76,000 at the high in October 2025. Every one of the seven watched half of it disappear in a year. No day of the week saved anybody from that.

So stop hunting for the magic day. The best day of the week to buy Bitcoin is the one you will not skip.

Common questions

What is the best day of the week to buy Bitcoin?

No day wins reliably. In a five-year test of $100 weekly buys, the best day (Saturday) beat the worst day (Wednesday) by $123 on $26,100 invested.

Is Bitcoin cheaper on weekends?

Not in a way you can count on. Saturday was the best day to buy in the five-year test, but it came in last when the same test was run back to 2017.

Is Monday a good day to buy Bitcoin?

Monday has the biggest average gain of any day since 2015, but Bitcoin finished higher on only 53% of Mondays. The Monday buyer finished fifth out of seven in the five-year test.

Does it matter what day you DCA into Bitcoin?

Barely. Even buying on the cheapest day of every single week for five years would have added only about 3%. Fees and skipped weeks cost far more than the day you pick.
