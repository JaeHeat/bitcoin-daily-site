A celebrity coin is a crypto token launched by a famous person, or pushed to their followers in posts and interviews. The pitch is always the same. A big name, a fast chart, and the feeling that you are early. This tracker follows every one we have verified, from its all-time high to today, so you can see how that story usually ends.

The pattern repeats with very few exceptions. Most of these coins hit their highest price within days of launching, while the attention was at its peak. Then the selling started and never really stopped. On several of them a large share of the supply sat with the team or early insiders from day one, and the people who bought first could sell into the excitement. Everyone who bought the headline paid for it.

Read the tracker as a warning, not a list of bargains. A coin that is down 99% can still fall another 99%. A coin that bounces is not a coin that has come back. The useful question before the next celebrity launch is not how high it can go. It is who already owns it, and who they are selling to.

Each coin is listed with the celebrity who launched it or publicly promoted it, and copycat tokens that only borrow a famous name are left out. The peak is the all-time high recorded by CoinMarketCap, or by CoinGecko for coins CoinMarketCap no longer tracks. Prices refresh every day, and new coins are added once the celebrity's role is confirmed.
