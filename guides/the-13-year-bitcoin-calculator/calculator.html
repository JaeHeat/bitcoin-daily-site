What the original $60 example measures

GTA V's U.S. standard edition was $59.99. At the September 17, 2013 BTC daily close of $126.92, that same money represents 0.47265994 BTC.

At the dated $62,064 reference, the holding is worth $29,335.17. The video calls that nearly $30,000.

That result included deep drawdowns. The guide shows the holding's values through the 2013–2015, 2017–2018 and 2021–2022 declines, plus the shrinking multiples between recorded highs.

How the calculations work

  • BTC quantity = dollars allocated ÷ BTC purchase price.
  • Future value = BTC quantity × future BTC price.
  • Annual growth required = (future price ÷ purchase price)1 / years − 1.
  • Today's purchasing power = future value ÷ (1 + inflation rate)years.

Real prices would not follow a smooth growth curve. A zero future price means a total loss of the allocation. Future taxes and fees are not included.

This is a scenario calculator, not a fitted forecasting model or a personalized allocation recommendation.
